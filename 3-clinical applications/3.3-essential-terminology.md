## 📘 Terms Covered
- Anesthesia  
- Diagnosis  
- Prognosis  
- Comorbidity  
- Etiology  

---

## 🧾 Definitions & Breakdown

| Term | IPA (Pronunciation) | Meaning | Breakdown | Example in Context |
|------|----------------------|---------|-----------|--------------------|
| **Anesthesia** | /ˌæn.əsˈθiː.ʒə/ | Medication that prevents pain during surgery/procedures | an- (without) + esthesia (sensation) | "The patient received general anesthesia before the cardiac procedure" |
| **Diagnosis** | /ˌdaɪ.əɡˈnoʊ.sɪs/ | The identification of a disease or condition | dia- (through) + gnosis (knowledge) | "The primary diagnosis code is I50.9 for heart failure" |
| **Prognosis** | /prɑɡˈnoʊ.sɪs/ | Predicted outcome or course of a disease | pro- (before) + gnosis (knowledge) | "Machine learning models can help predict patient prognosis" |
| **Comorbidity** | /ˌkoʊ.mɔrˈbɪd.ə.t̬i/ | Having two or more diseases simultaneously | co- (together) + morbid (disease) + -ity (condition) | "Diabetes comorbidity increases surgical risk scores" |
| **Etiology** | /ˌiː.t̬iˈɑː.lə.dʒi/ | The cause or origin of a disease | etio- (cause) + -logy (study of) | "Understanding disease etiology improves predictive models" |

## 💡 Real-World Data Science Examples
- **Anesthesia**: Appears in procedure codes (CPT), affects length of stay analysis, critical for surgical outcome models  
- **Diagnosis**: Primary/secondary diagnosis codes (ICD-10), foundation of all medical analytics and risk stratification  
- **Prognosis**: Target variable in many ML models, survival analysis, readmission prediction  
- **Comorbidity**: Key feature in risk adjustment models, CMS-HCC coding, Charlson Comorbidity Index  
- **Etiology**: Important for causal inference models, epidemiological studies, root cause analysis
