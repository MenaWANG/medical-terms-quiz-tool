# Respiratory System - Common Symptoms & Procedures

## 📘 Terms Covered
- Asthma (airway inflammation)
- Pneumonia (lung infection)
- Dyspnea (difficulty breathing)
- Bronchitis (bronchi inflammation)
- Pneumectomy (lung removal)
- Bronchoscopy (airway examination)
- Tachypnea (rapid breathing)

## 📝 Quick Quiz

1. What does the prefix "dys-" in dyspnea indicate?
2. Which term describes inflammation of the main airways in the lungs?
3. What is the medical term for abnormally fast breathing?
4. What does pneumectomy involve?
5. Which condition involves chronic airway inflammation and narrowing?

<details>
<summary>💡 Answers</summary>

1. **Dys-** means difficult or abnormal, so dyspnea means difficult breathing
2. **Bronchitis** - inflammation (-itis) of the bronchi (main airways)
3. **Tachypnea** - tachy (fast) + pnea (breathing)
4. **Pneumectomy** involves surgical removal (-ectomy) of lung tissue (pneumo-)
5. **Asthma** involves chronic airway inflammation and narrowing causing breathing difficulties

</details>

## 💡 Real-World Example

- **Code**: J44.1 - Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease with acute exacerbation
- **Description**: Patient presents with acute worsening of chronic lung disease
- **Breakdown**: 
  - Chronic = long-term condition
  - Obstructive = blocking airflow
  - Pulmonary = relating to lungs
  - Exacerbation = worsening of symptoms
- **Interpretation**: A patient with long-term lung disease (like COPD) experiencing a sudden worsening of their breathing problems, often requiring immediate medical attention and treatment adjustments.
